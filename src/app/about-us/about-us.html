The Story Behind Our Name: Unique Reach Media

We started as a small, passionate digital marketing crew — no fancy titles, no big setups. Just a tight-knit team of creatives, marketers, and strategists who loved what we did and gave it everything — from pitching late at night to designing campaigns early in the morning. We were pushing boundaries, finding clients one call at a time, helping brands get seen, and slowly building our name in the digital space. Then, something amazing happened. One of our biggest clients — Unique Hire, a globally recognized brand with a solid footprint across industries — took notice. Not just of our work, but of how we worked. The ideas. The dedication. The results. It wasn’t long before they approached us with an offer that changed everything. “You guys are killing it. What if we gave you a whole new wing to do it even bigger and better?” That moment was our turning point. And just like that, Unique Reach Media was born — the digital marketing extension of Unique Hire, but with our own identity, our own team, and our own vision. A new name, but the same passion. Today, we’re proud to be the "unique" part of a much larger story. We’re not just riding the wave — we’re helping create it. From working out of a shared desk to serving brands across industries, we’ve come a long way — and we’re only getting started. We’re here to help you grow — with bold ideas, smart strategies, and campaigns that truly deliver.
